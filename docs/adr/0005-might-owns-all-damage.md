# Might owns all damage magnitude; Intellect governs AoE only, not a spell-power track

The core attribute table originally listed Intellect as governing "Area of effect and damage," which conflicts with Might's "damage and healing" — two attributes both claiming to scale damage magnitude. Three ways to resolve it were considered: drop "damage" from Intellect's row as vestigial wording; keep it purely as an emergent effect of hitting more targets with a wider AoE (no separate formula); or give Intellect a real second multiplicative damage modifier for AoE-tagged abilities.

We went with the vestigial reading: **magic power folds entirely into Might's existing unbounded damage/healing curve.** There is no separate spell-power stat — physical and magical damage are the same Might-scaled number regardless of source. Intellect's role is corrected to AoE radius/target-count only; any extra total damage from a wide AoE is just the natural consequence of hitting more targets, not a second curve.

Consequence, worth calling out because it cuts against the RPG default: a high-Intellect, low-Might spellcaster will *not* hit hard. Dealing significant damage — magical or physical — always requires investing in Might. Intellect investment buys reach (AoE size) and utility (Cost Reduction), not raw power.
